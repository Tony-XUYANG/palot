# Palot Cloud Service Terms and Privacy Notice (Draft)

This document is an engineering draft and must be reviewed for the approved merchant entity,
jurisdiction, tax treatment, privacy obligations, and payment-provider rules before publication.

## Service credits

- Palot Cloud credits purchase access to DeepSeek and GLM inference supplied through Palot Cloud.
- Credits are not currency, stored-value money, an investment, or a transferable digital asset.
- Credits cannot be transferred, traded, withdrawn, or exchanged for cash and do not auto-renew.
- Codex, OpenAI, and other BYOK providers are billed separately by their providers.

## Charges and refunds

- Each checkout displays the exact CNY payment and credit amount before the customer pays.
- Usage is charged from the active published model-price version using provider token usage when
  available and a disclosed conservative estimate only when usage is unavailable.
- Failed requests are refunded automatically. Unfinished reservations expire and are released.
- During the beta, an unused top-up may be requested for an original-route refund. Consumed credit
  is not refundable except where required by applicable law or caused by a Palot billing error.

## Data handling

- Palot stores account identifiers, token hashes, balances, sanitized usage totals, payment order
  identifiers, amounts, states, and cryptographic callback hashes.
- Palot does not store payment-card data, complete payment callbacks, model prompts, source code,
  model responses, authorization headers, or upstream model keys in the payment database or logs.
- Merchant keys and model-provider keys are restricted to encrypted cloud secrets and are never
  distributed in the desktop application.

## Availability and support

- Service may be interrupted by model providers, payment providers, networks, or maintenance.
- Accounts may be frozen to investigate fraud, reconciliation differences, chargebacks, or abuse.
- The final published notice must identify the merchant entity, support channel, effective date,
  refund contact, data retention periods, and applicable dispute process.
